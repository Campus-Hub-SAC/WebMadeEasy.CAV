❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build my website?
+
Your website will be ready in 3–4 working days from the day we finalize the content and requirements. Rush delivery may be available on request.
Can you build websites for non-educational businesses?
+
Absolutely! While I specialize in schools and colleges, I build websites for any type of business — boutiques, restaurants, clinics, startups, NGOs, and more.
What about domain & hosting — do I need to buy them separately?
+
The Subdomain plan includes free hosting via Cloudflare Pages — no separate purchase needed. The Custom Domain plan requires a domain (around ₹800–1,200/year), but I'll help you set it all up seamlessly.
Is the monthly fee compulsory?
+
The ₹400–500/month maintenance fee covers your hosting, SSL, and ongoing support. It's optional for static sites, but recommended to keep your site updated and performing well.
What is the Google Verified badge?
+
The Google Verified badge adds a credibility indicator to your website, showing visitors that your business identity has been verified. It helps build trust and is available as an add-on for just ₹200 one-time.
How many revisions do I get?
+
All plans include free revisions during the project. After launch, you get 2 edits per month as part of the maintenance package. Major redesigns are quoted separately.
