Welcome to Sunrise Public School, Bangalore

Located in India’s Silicon Valley, we blend traditional values with modern pedagogy to ignite curiosity, creativity, and character in every child. Explore our vibrant campus and discover why we are one of the top CBSE schools in the region.

About Our School

Discover the vision, infrastructure, and values behind Sunrise Public School.

🏫 Our Campus

8‑acre green campus with smart classrooms, advanced labs, a library with 20,000+ books, and a 600‑seat auditorium.

🎯 Vision & Mission

To nurture compassionate, curious leaders who excel academically while staying rooted in Indian values.

📚 CBSE Curriculum

We follow the CBSE syllabus with a focus on conceptual learning, practicals, and holistic assessment.

Our Faculty Excellence

Our 85+ highly qualified teachers are the backbone of Sunrise. With a student‑teacher ratio of 14:1, every child receives personalised attention. All educators undergo regular NEP‑aligned training.
