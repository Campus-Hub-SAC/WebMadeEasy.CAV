OUR STORY

About Sunrise Public School

Sunrise Public School was established in the year 2000 with a vision to provide quality education that goes beyond textbooks. Our institution has grown from a small school with 3 classrooms to a sprawling 5-acre campus with world-class infrastructure.

We follow the CBSE curriculum and are known for our student-centric approach to learning. Our dedicated team of 85+ qualified educators ensures that every child receives personalized attention and guidance.

Our alumni include doctors, engineers, artists, entrepreneurs, and public figures who have made us proud on national and international platforms.
